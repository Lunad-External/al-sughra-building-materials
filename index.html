Frequently Asked Questions about Building Materials in Sharjah & the UAE

Answers to common questions from contractors, fit-out companies and builders about our products, bulk supply and delivery.

What building materials does Al Sughra Building Materials Trading LLC supply in Sharjah?

+

We supply five main product ranges for construction and interior decoration: tiling and flooring accessories, drywall, plastering and ceiling systems, concrete and masonry accessories, sealing, jointing and protection materials, and scaffolding and architectural products. Our range includes tile trims, stair nosing and tile leveling systems, plastering beads, fiberglass mesh, ceiling tiles and gypsum partition accessories, PVC rebar spacers, chamfers, waterstops and glass blocks, PE backing rod and tapes, PU decorative products, and aluminium scaffolding and ladders.

Do you supply building materials in bulk for contractors and construction projects?

+

Yes. We are a wholesale building materials supplier serving contractors, construction companies, fit-out firms, interior designers and traders. With 3 warehouses and 3 branch offices, we hold stock for bulk and project orders and offer volume pricing for large quantities.

Do you deliver building materials across the UAE?

+

Yes. We deliver from our Sharjah warehouses to project sites across the UAE, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Ajman and the Northern Emirates, with free delivery on bulk orders. We also supply customers across the wider Middle East.

Which tile accessories and tile trims do you stock?

+

Our tiling and flooring range includes aluminium expansion cover joints, carpet profiles, stair nosing, tile leveling systems and tile trims for tile installation, floor transitions and finishing in residential, commercial and industrial projects.

Where is your building materials store in Sharjah located?

+

Our main location is on Maliha Road, opposite Al Ghurair Exchange, Sharjah, UAE. We operate 3 warehouses and 3 branch offices, all with ample parking for customers and trucks.
